Stock Market Today: Dow Up, Nasdaq Falls As AI Names Sandisk, Western Digital Sink After Earnings (Live Coverage)
The Dow Jones Industrial Average posted gains while the Nasdaq declined as disappointing earnings from AI-linked storage companies Sandisk and Western Digital dragged on the tech-heavy index. Both companies reported results that fell short of market expectations, triggering sharp drops in their share prices. The divergence between the Dow and Nasdaq reflects continued rotation away from high-valuation technology names.
Sherrill unveils first set of midterm endorsements
New Jersey Governor Mikie Sherrill launched her federal political action committee, Mission to Deliver, and announced endorsements for 13 congressional Democratic candidates with military or national security backgrounds. The move is part of a broader Democratic Party effort to build support ahead of the 2026 midterm elections. Sherrill is positioning the PAC as a vehicle to elevate candidates whose profiles align with national security priorities.
Fed Governor Cook says she's 'prepared to act' on rate hike to address inflation
Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook stated she is prepared to support an interest rate increase if inflation warrants further action. Cook was among the nine members who voted last week to hold the benchmark borrowing rate steady in a range of 3.5% to 3.75%, with three members dissenting. Her comments signal that additional rate hikes remain on the table as the Fed continues to monitor inflation data.
China's Robots Know Kung Fu and Can Scale Walls. Can They Woo Investors?
Chinese robotics firm Unitree Robotics is seeking to raise approximately $900 million through an initial public offering, testing investor appetite for humanoid robot technology. The company has gained attention for robots capable of impressive physical feats, including martial arts movements and wall-climbing. The IPO will serve as a significant test of whether the humanoid robotics sector can translate technological spectacle into a commercially viable and investable business.
