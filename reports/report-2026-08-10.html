Shares of Monday.com fell following the report, with the disappointing outlook outweighing the headline earnings beat.
Stocks Churn as Oil Advance Boosts Treasury Yields: Markets Wrap
A renewed rise in oil prices pushed Treasury yields higher, leaving equity markets unsettled at the start of the trading week. Higher energy costs can feed into inflation expectations, which in turn pressure bond prices and lift yields. Stocks wavered in choppy trading as investors grew cautious ahead of key inflation data due later in the week.
Stock market today: Dow, S&P 500, Nasdaq little changed in wait for key inflation data
The Dow Jones Industrial Average, S&P 500, and Nasdaq all posted minimal moves on Monday as markets entered a holding pattern. Investors are awaiting upcoming inflation reports that could influence the Federal Reserve's next policy decisions. The subdued session reflected broad caution across Wall Street with little conviction in either direction ahead of the data releases.
Berkshire earnings rose last quarter and CEO Greg Abel is starting to deploy Buffett's massive cash hoard
Berkshire Hathaway reported higher earnings last quarter, driven by strength in its energy, railroad, and manufacturing divisions that more than offset weaker insurance results. The conglomerate had accumulated a historically large cash reserve under longtime CEO Warren Buffett before Greg Abel assumed leadership. Abel has begun deploying that capital, signaling a more active investment posture under the company's new chief executive.
