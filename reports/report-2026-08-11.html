Oil in US Strategic Petroleum Reserve falls to lowest level since 1983
U.S. Strategic Petroleum Reserve stockpiles fell by 6.1 million barrels to 298.7 million barrels, the lowest level recorded since January 1983. The drawdown has been driven by ongoing supply strains tied to Middle East tensions affecting regional oil flows. The declining reserve level raises questions about the government's buffer capacity should energy supply disruptions intensify further.
Former Biden adviser blasts Trump vaccine move: Don't want to be like Tajikistan
President Trump signed an executive order Monday directing that the combined measles, mumps, and rubella vaccine be administered as three separate shots across multiple doctor's visits rather than as a single MMR dose. Dr. Ashish Jha, who served as the Biden administration's White House COVID-19 coordinator, publicly criticized the move, warning it could reduce vaccination compliance and undermine public health outcomes. The order marks a significant departure from longstanding U.S. immunization guidelines supported by major medical organizations.
